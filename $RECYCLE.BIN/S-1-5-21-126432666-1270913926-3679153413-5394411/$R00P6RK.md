# Introduction and scope

The objective of this cookbook is to guide deployment of the lifecycle assessment (LCA) feature into TcX environment. This includes details about required steps and configurations to enable Sustainability related functionality on both Teamcenter and at the Integration layer (T4SUST).

Scope includes: 
- Enabling the LCA within Teamcenter.
- Configuring the Integration layer (T4SUST) to support communication with LCA provider 
- Post deployment configurations for both Teamcenter and T4SUST
- Configurations to ensure data exchange between Teamcenter and LCA provider
- Ensure communication between Teamcenter and LCA provider for computing LCA scores
