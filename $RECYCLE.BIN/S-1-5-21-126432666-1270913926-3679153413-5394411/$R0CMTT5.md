**1.6** Human Resources Security
Policies and requirements must be implemented to promote the recruitment of competent, qualified individuals,
ensuring the organization and user's responsibilities within the context of information security are communicated
and accepted in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Information Security Human
Resources Policy.
  - **1.6.1** Processes must be implemented for the screening of candidates for employment in accordance with
relevant laws, regulations, and ethics. Implemented processes for screening are commensurate with business
requirements and the classification of the information resources to be accessed.
  - **1.6.2** Processes must be implemented to ensure candidates are made aware of the terms and conditions of
employment, during the screening process, employment, and following employment. These processes ensure
candidates are made aware of SISW information resource security responsibilities and user security
responsibilities.
  - **1.6.3** Organizational structures must be established and define lines of reporting.
  - **1.6.4** A formal disciplinary action process must be implemented and established.
  - **1.6.5** Awareness education and training must be provided and regular updates in organizational policies
and requirements, as relevant for their job function are communicated to SISW personnel.
  - **1.6.6** Processes must be implemented for the creation, review, approval, and communication of an
established policy on information security which acts as the foundation of the security awareness program.
  - **1.6.7** Processes must be implemented to ensure employees read and acknowledge their understanding of
SISW's established policy on information security annually, including SISW personnel roles and responsibilities
relating to information security.
  - **1.6.8** Processes must be implemented for the termination of employment in accordance with relevant
contractual, legal, environmental, and business requirements.
  - **1.6.9** Periodic security training that is adequate to advise SISW personnel on information security best
practices must be conducted, including those specific to SISW.
  - **1.6.10** Processes must be implemented for the collection, review, and actioning of metrics relating to SISW
personnel participation in security awareness program activities.
  - **1.6.11** An acceptable use of information resources consistent with information resource classification
guidelines and related information security requirements is required.
