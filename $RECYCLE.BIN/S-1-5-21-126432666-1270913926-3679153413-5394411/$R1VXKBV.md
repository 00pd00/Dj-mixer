SISW Saas Network Security Policy requirements
  - **1.1** Processes must be implemented to ensure information security related requirements relating to network
security are included in the requirements for new information systems or enhancements to existing information
systems.
  - **1.2** Standards and the associated technical and administrative networking controls must be implemented to
facilitate teleworking.
  - **1.3** Administrative and technical controls must be implemented to ensure networks are managed and controlled
to protect information within SISW information resources.
    - **1.3.1** Data transferred from information resources on the SISW network must be protected in accordance
with the established requirements on data privacy and information security.
    - **1.3.2** Standards and the associated technical and administrative controls must be implemented to facilitate
access, authentication, authorization and management of network devices and network services.
  - **1.4** Segmentation in networks must be established to facilitate segregation of information resources consistent
with contractual, legal, environmental, and business requirements.
    - **1.4.1** Physical and logical network segmentation must be established to facilitate segregation of
information resources consistent with contractual, legal, environmental, and business requirements.
    - **1.4.2** By default, network connections must be configured in such a way to deny traffic unless it is explicitly
allowed for a necessary purpose.
    - **1.4.3** Network security architecture must be documented (e.g., via network diagram) and periodically
reviewed and updated for revisions.
    - **1.4.4** Network security configurations, rules and permissions established within network devices (e.g.,
Firewalls, WAF), virtual environment gateways (e.g., Load Balancer, Security Groups, Transit gateway) or other
configurable element(s) (e.g., IP tables, endpoint firewalls) must be reviewed at least annually, and results of
the review documented and approved by the asset owner.
    - **1.4.5** Redundancies in network communication must be established to manage outages in accordance with
an established policy on business continuity management.
    - **1.4.6** SI SW must maintain segregation between internal and customer accessible environment
    - **1.4.7** Segregation of tenants in a multi-tenant customer accessible environment must be enforced via the
implementation of appropriate controls as a minimum, at the:
• Application Layer
• Network Layer
• Data handling
• Data at rest layer
  - **1.5** Standards and the associated technical and administrative controls must be implemented to facilitate the
protection of information passing over public networks from fraudulent activity, contract dispute, or unauthorized
disclosure and modification.
    - **1.5.1** Administrative and technical controls must be implemented to ensure the SISW network is managed
and controlled in a way that protects information resources transferred across the network.
    - **1.5.2** Technical controls must be implemented to protect wireless access, including but not limited to
established authentication protocols, ensuring authorized access, encryption techniques, default keys, network
filtering, and monitoring for rogue access points.
