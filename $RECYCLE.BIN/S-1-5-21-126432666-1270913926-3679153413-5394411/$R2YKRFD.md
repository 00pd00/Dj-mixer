# Dispatcher Installation Prerequisites

Before you begin installing the dispatcher, make sure that DC Services are running.

## How to Start DC Services

1. Refer to the instructions in the section [Commands to Start and Stop DC Services on Linux](../../020_Operations/030_Day%20N%20Operations/110_Stop%20DC%20Services.md#commands-to-start-and-stop-dc-services-on-linux).
2. Confirm that the DC Services have started successfully before proceeding with the dispatcher installation.
