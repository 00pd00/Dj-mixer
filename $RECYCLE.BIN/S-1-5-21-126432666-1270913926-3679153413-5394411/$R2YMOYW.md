## Product Overview
Product Configurator provides definition of product variability in terms of Families and Features across the entire product lifecycle.
Constraints help scope down variability for specific product models or variants and allow to define valid or invalid feature combinations.
Product Configurator supports filtering of structured content from any connected application, within Teamcenter as well as connected applications.
