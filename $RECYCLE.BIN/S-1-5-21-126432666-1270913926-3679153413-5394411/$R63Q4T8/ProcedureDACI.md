# ProcedureDACI

__DACI for ISMS Compliance in TcX Development__

Sometimes clearer than a RACI when it comes to getting things done, a DACI chart has the following elements:

__Driver – __the person who organizes the effort, assigns work, etc

__Approver__ – the person who accepts or approves of the result and is __held accountable__ for the quality

__Contributor__ – the persons who do the work

__Informed__ – the persons who receive the information without directly contributing
