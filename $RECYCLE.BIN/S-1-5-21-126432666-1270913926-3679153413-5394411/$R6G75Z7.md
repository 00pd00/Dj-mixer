## Tenant Onboarding Considerations

When onboarding a tenant, assess whether logs from specific Azure Managed Resources in that tenant environment should be sent to Datadog. If log forwarding is required, follow the instructions in the [Azure Log Forwarding Setup Guide](../../000_Cell-Setup/000_Automation%20Prerequisites/040_TcX%20Cell%20Setup/AZURE/100_Setup%20Datadog%20in%20Admin%20subscription/120_Azure%20Log%20Forwarding.md).
