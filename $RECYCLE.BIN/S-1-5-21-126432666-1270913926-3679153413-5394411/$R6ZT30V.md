# Power BI License Requirement

**Responsibility: The Customer Azure Admin team is responsible for ensuring required licenses are available in customer tenants.**

A Power BI license is essential to publish, share, and collaborate on reports and dashboards within the Power BI Service, enabling advanced features and secure content distribution across your organization.


Before proceeding with the Power BI integration setup, ensure that the user has one of the following PowerBI license assigned to his account:
- Premium license (PPU Premium Per User, Fabric Trial, or Fabric Capacity) to enable publishing of PowerBI reports.


![alt text](PowerBI_License.png)


⚠️ Note: Users without the appropriate license will not be able to publish reports in the Power BI service.

**The required Power BI license type also depends on the volume of data being handled, as each license has its own data capacity and performance limitations.**
