**1.10** Logging and Monitoring
Information resource logs must be managed, and security events must be monitored in accordance with the
Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Security Logging and Monitoring Policy.
  - **1.10.1** Processes must be implemented for determining the log information to capture from applicable
information resources.
  - **1.10.2** Processes must be implemented for defining and categorizing security events utilizing a risk-based
approach to assess the likelihood and impact of security events on information resources.
  - **1.10.3** Standards detailing the required logging capabilities must be implemented to be enabled on
information resources to facilitate monitoring and actioning of identified security events.
  - **1.10.4** Standards must be established to facilitate retention and disposal of logs consistent with contractual,
legal, environmental, and business requirements.
  - **1.10.5** Standards defining incident response criteria must be established to facilitate actions required for
defined and identified security events in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Incident
Management Policy.
  - **1.10.6** Administrative and technical controls must be implemented to restrict access to audit logging facilities
and the associated logs.
  - **1.10.7** All systems and applications must be continuously synchronized with a trusted time server to ensure
consistency of timestamps amongst information resource logs from all in-scope systems or applications.

**1.11** Network Security
The Siemens SISW network must be protected through a secure network infrastructure, network access controls,
and information transfer requirements in accordance with the Siemens Information Software (SISW) Saas Network
Security Policy.
  - **1.11.1** Processes must be implemented to ensure information security related requirements relating to
network security are included in the requirements for new information systems or enhancements to existing
information systems.
  - **1.11.2** Standards and the associated technical and administrative networking controls must be implemented
to facilitate teleworking.
  - **1.11.3** Administrative and technical controls must be implemented to ensure networks are managed and
controlled to protect information with SISW information resources.
  - **1.11.4** Segmentation in networks must be established to facilitate segregation of information resources
consistent with contractual, legal, environmental, and business requirements.
  - **1.11.5** Standards and the associated technical and administrative controls must be implemented to facilitate
the protection of information passing over public networks should be protected from fraudulent activity,
contract dispute, or unauthorized disclosure and modification.
  - **1.11.6** Software installation must be restricted on Company operational systems, including mobile devices
and laptops, through the implementation of change management and user access management processes and
technical controls.
  - **1.11.7** Formal documented policies and requirements must be implemented to facilitate governing the
installation of software by users.
  - **1.11.8** Standards must be established to guide personnel on the types of software installations that are
permitted and what types of installations are prohibited on information resources.
