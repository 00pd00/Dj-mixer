**1.7** Incident Management
Information security incidents must be managed, including detection, analysis, resolution, and lessons learned in
accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Incident Management Policy.
  - **1.7.1** Formal documented policies and requirements must be implemented to facilitate incident response
planning and preparation, monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and reporting information security events and
incidents, logging incident management activities, assessment of and decision on information security events/
assessment of information security weaknesses, response requirements including those for escalation,
controlled recovery from an incident, and communication to internal and external people or organizations.
  - **1.7.2** Formal reporting requirements must be implemented to facilitate consistent reporting actions when
information security events occur.
  - **1.7.3** Formal disciplinary processes must be implemented for dealing with employees who commit security
breaches.
  - **1.7.4** Formal feedback processes must be implemented to ensure that those persons reporting information
security events must be notified of results after the issue has been resolved and closed.
  - **1.7.5** Processes must be implemented to ensure the objectives for information security incident
management are agreed with by management.
  - **1.7.6** Processes must be implemented that communicate organizational priorities for handling information
security incidents to employees with incident response obligations.

**1.8** Information Resource Management
Information resources must be securely managed by identification, inventorying, maintenance, and protection
controls in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Information Resource Management Policy.
  - **1.8.1** Requirements must be implemented that facilitate the tracking of information resources as part of an
asset inventory.
  - **1.8.2** Requirements must be implemented that facilitate the definition assignment of ownership of
information resources.
  - **1.8.3** Requirements must be implemented that facilitate the standards for the acceptable use of
information resources in accordance with an established policy on human resources security.
  - **1.8.4** Standards must be established for the return of information resources upon termination of
employment, contract or agreement.
  - **1.8.5** Standards must be established for the classification, labeling, and handling of information resources.
  - **1.8.6** Requirements must be established for the management of removable media in accordance with the
classification standards.
  - **1.8.7** Requirements must be implemented to facilitate defining data retention, decommissioning, and
disposal protection requirements consistent with contractual, legal, environmental, and business requirements.
  - **1.8.8** Mobile devices that are owned and operated by employees in the course of business must be
protected (i.e., bring-your-own-device (BYOD) devices) from theft and unintended use by establishing end user
agreements with personnel and measures to separate SISW data from personal data on such devices.
  - **1.8.9** Processes must be implemented for the management of BYOD devices that access SISW information
resources.
  - **1.8.10** Processes must be established for the information security management of end point devices and
removable media.
  - **1.8.11** Processes and procedures must be established to facilitate the installation of software by SISW
employees and contractors under controlled circumstances.

**1.9** Information Security Risk Management
Information security, personnel and business risks related to information resources must be identified, assessed,
and managed in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Information Security Risk
Management Policy.
  - **1.9.1** An Information Security Council \{ISC) is established to facilitate the development, review, and
approval of requirements for the performance of risk assessment activities utilizing defined risk management
frameworks.
