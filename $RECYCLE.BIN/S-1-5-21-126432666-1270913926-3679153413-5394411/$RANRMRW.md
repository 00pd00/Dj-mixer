SISW Saas Information Security Policy requirements

**1.1** Access Control
Access will be restricted by access control, user access management, privileged access, access review processes,
along with multi-factor authentication and password requirements, where applicable, in accordance with the
Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Access Control Policy.
    - **1.1.1** Restriction of access to information resources and their associated functions must be established
based on information resource requirements and in accordance with a documented policy on access control.
Restriction of access must be extended to data stores or storage mechanisms, and where possible applied on
documents or information directly, to reduce opportunities for data exfiltration.
    - **1.1.2** User registration and de-registration processes must be established to enable assignment of access
rights to information resources based on authorization.
    - **1.1.3** Administrative user registration and de-registration processes must be documented and established
for privileged access rights.
    - **1.1.4** Authentication credentials for management of user and system information resource must be in
alignment with industry best practices.
    - **1.1.5** User access rights must be reviewed at periodic intervals.
    - **1.1.6** Processes for the removal or modification of user and system access rights must be established based
on user status changes (termination/modification of employment, contract or agreement) or information
resource changes.
    - **1.1.7** A separation of roles must exist within the user access management lifecycle.

**1.2** Information Security Business Continuity Management
Information security aspects of business continuity must be managed to support the availability of information
resources and support capacity management requirements in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software
(SISW) Saas Business Continuity Management Policy.
1.2.lProcesses must be implemented, and requirements must be established for information security and the
continuity of information security management in adverse situations to support established SLAs or
commitments of the organization.
  - **1.2.2** Processes, requirements, and controls must be documented, implemented, and maintained to ensure
the required level of continuity for information security during an adverse situation.
  - **1.2.3** Requirements must be implemented to ensure information security continuity controls are verified at
regular intervals to assess their validity and effectiveness during adverse situations.
