
##### Get access to `tcx_cli` CLI lambda for TcX Tenant Administrative account

This step is a one-time requirement for each new TcX Tenant Administrative Account during onboarding. The `tcx_cli` Lambda function is essential for retrieving the SAML secret within the tenant deployment pipeline.

To gain access to the `tcx_cli` Lambda function for your TcX Tenant Administrative Account, please follow the process outlined below:
