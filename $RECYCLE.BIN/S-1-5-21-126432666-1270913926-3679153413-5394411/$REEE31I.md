  - **1.3** Supplier Relationship Management
  - **1.3.1** Supplier relationship owners for each supplier must be identified.
  - **1.3.2** Relationship owners must liaise on customer or supplier issues and ensure the quality of the relationship is
based on trust & transparency (e.g., through service level agreements).
  - **1.3.3** SISW must consider how to change or stop the use of cloud services, if needed.

**1.4** Supplier Risk Management
  - **1.4.1** Contracts must conform to legal and regulatory requirements and must clearly state service goals and
expected deliverables.
  - **1.4.2** Risks relating to suppliers' ability to continue effective service delivery to SISW in a secure and efficient
manner on an ongoing basis must be identified and mitigated.
  - **1.4.3** Through the application of risk management disciplines, SISW must further consider non-disclosure
agreements (NDAs), escrow contracts, and continued supplier viability, conformance with security requirements,
alternative suppliers, penalties and rewards.

**1.5** Supplier Service Delivery
  - **1.5.1** A process to monitor service delivery must be established to ensure the supplier is meeting SISW's current
business requirements and is continuing to adhere to the contract agreements and service level agreements.
  - **1.5.2** This process must also help ensure that performance is competitive with alternative suppliers and in market
conditions.
