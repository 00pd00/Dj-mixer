### Prerequisites

#### cTcX Tenants

Two cTcX Tenants are required. They are referred to as site1 and site2 here.

Tenant referred to as site1 (or OEM Sponsor Site henceforward) must have **Supplier Connect for Sponsor** solution installed (Product Id - TC030911-XT). 

Tenant referred to as site2 (or OEM Supplier Site henceforward) must have **Supplier Connect for Supplier** solution installed (Product Id - TC030914-XT). 

Configure Multi-Site Collaboration between the OEM Sponsor Site and the OEM Supplier Site as specified in the cTcX cookbook section [Multi-Site Deployment](../Multi-Site%20Deployment/Multi-Site%20Deployment)
