# Introduction

> ℹ️ Supported in TcX version 2606 and above

Teamcenter Copilot BOM Add-on software for Teamcenter X Product ID - TC030507-XT

## Teamcenter Copilot for Structures

Teamcenter Copilot for Structures allows your users to quickly find, open, filter, and update a structure and the visual representation. It also helps your users create worksets, complete weight and balance rollups, and replace parts.

## Dependency

Teamcenter Copilot BOM Add-on leverages the infrastructure and resources provisioned by [Teamcenter AI Chat](../Teamcenter%20AI%20Chat/000_TC%20AI%20Introduction/010_Introduction.md) (Product ID: TC030406-XT). Teamcenter AI Chat must be installed and fully configured as a prerequisite before deploying the Copilot BOM Add-on. Ensure that TC030406-XT is included in the product selection during installation.
