
#### Activating a PIM Role/Group

1. Search and open `Privileged Identity Management` in top search bar.
2. Select `My Roles` from the `Tasks` group in left navigation.

    ![My roles](image_9.png)

3. Navigate to Role/Group tab.

    **Note:** Direct links:
        - Roles: [https://portal.azure.com/?feature.msaljs=true#view/Microsoft_Azure_PIMCommon/ActivationMenuBlade/~/aadmigratedroles](https://portal.azure.com/?feature.msaljs=true#view/Microsoft_Azure_PIMCommon/ActivationMenuBlade/~/aadmigratedroles)
        - Groups: [https://portal.azure.com/?feature.msaljs=true#view/Microsoft_Azure_PIMCommon/ActivationMenuBlade/~/aadgroup](https://portal.azure.com/?feature.msaljs=true#view/Microsoft_Azure_PIMCommon/ActivationMenuBlade/~/aadgroup)

4. Click `Activate` against the desired Role/Group.

    ![groups](image_7.png)

5. Enter a reason and select `Activate`.

    ![activate](image_8.png)

6. Wait on the same page till the role activates and the page refreshes.
