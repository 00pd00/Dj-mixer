**1.3** Change Management
Formal change management requirements must be followed when introducing new information resources and
making changes to existing information resources in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas
Change Management Policy.
  - **1.3.1** Formal responsibilities must be implemented to facilitate the control of changes.
  - **1.3.2** Documented requirements to facilitate change requests, prioritization, review, approval, testing, and
implementation of changes must be in place.
  - **1.3.3** Changes must be monitored during and after implementation.

**1.4** Compliance
Compliance with security policies, standards, and any other relevant security requirements (i.e. legislative
statutory, regulatory, and/or contractual) must be reviewed in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software
(SISW) Saas Information Security Compliance Policy.
  - **1.4.1** Processes must be implemented to facilitate the identification, documentation, and maintenance of
relevant legislative, statutory, regulatory, contractual requirements, and the organization's approach to meet
these requirements.
  - **1.4.2** Processes must be implemented to facilitate protection of records from loss, destruction, falsification,
unauthorized access, and unauthorized release, in accordance with legislative, statutory, regulatory,
contractual, and business requirements.
  - **1.4.3** Requirements must be implemented to facilitate the privacy and protection of personally identifiable
information as required in relevant legislation, regulation, and contracts.
  - **1.4.4** Requirements must be implemented to facilitate the application of cryptographic controls in
compliance with all relevant agreements, legislation, and regulations.
  - **1.4.5** Processes must be implemented to facilitate independent reviews of the continuing suitability,
adequacy, and effectiveness of its approach to managing information security.
  - **1.4.6** Processes must be implemented to facilitate periodic area-specific technical and administrative
review of the compliance of information processing and requirements with applicable security policies and
standards.
  - **1.4.7** Policy exceptions shall be documented, reviewed, and approved using the exception process within
AMT.
    - **1.4.7.1** Policy exceptions shall only be submitted for review and approval into the AMT tool after
review with the Business Segment Leader.

**1.5** Data Privacy and Security
Information either generated by, or entrusted to, the care of SISW personnel will be classified with appropriate
protections to avoid unauthorized disclosure in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Data
Classification, Handling, and Sharing Policy.
  - **1.5.1** Processes must be implemented to ensure information resources are classified according to its legal
requirements, value, criticality, and sensitivity to SISW.
  - **1.5.2** All Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) referenced within applicable ISMS documents are
classified as Confidential information in accordance with Siemens Corporate Directives.
  - **1.5.3** Technical and administrative controls must be implemented to be consistent with information
resource classification.
  - **1.5.4** Requirements to facilitate consistent handling of information must be implemented according to its
legal requirements, value, criticality, and sensitivity.
  - **1.5.5** Processes must be implemented to facilitate the information resource lifecycle protection
commensurate with the information resource classification.
  - **1.5.6** Requirements must be implemented to facilitate handling of media according to its legal
requirements, value, criticality, and sensitivity.
  - **1.5.7** Requirements must be implemented to facilitate labeling of information resources in accordance with
information resource classification guidelines.
