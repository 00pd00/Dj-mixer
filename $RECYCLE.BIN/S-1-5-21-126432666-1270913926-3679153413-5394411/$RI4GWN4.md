Teamcenter AI Copilot PID is only supported in the below mentioned regions per cloud provider. Additional regions are currently unavailable due to CSO SCP restrictions in place. 

### Azure Supported Regions List 
  - eastus
  - eu central (Germany West Central)

### AWS Supported Regions List
  - us-east-1

> ℹ️ Deploying AI Copilot PID (TC030406-XT) in an unsupported region will cause pipeline failures.
