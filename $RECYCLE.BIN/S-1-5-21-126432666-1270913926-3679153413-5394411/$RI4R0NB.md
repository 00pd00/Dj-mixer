- **1.1.1** Requirements must be in place to facilitate the tracking of information resources onboarding, reuse and
disposal as part of an asset inventory system.
    - **1.1.2** Loss, theft, or other information security incident related to SISW information resources must be reported
immediately based on requirements in the incident management policy.
    - **1.1.3** Periodic review of all asset inventories for accuracy, completeness, and to ensure appropriate ownership
assignment and tracking.

**1.2** Requirements must be implemented that facilitate the definition assignment of ownership of information
resources.
    - **1.2.1** Standards must be established for the classification, labeling, and handling of information resources
based on their classification to protect from unauthorized disclosure or misuse.
    - **1.2.2** Requirements must be established for the management of mobile devices.
    - **1.2.3** Sensitive data must not be stored on mobile devices without encryption.

**1.3** Requirements must be implemented that facilitate the standards for the acceptable use of information
resources in accordance an established policy on human resources security.
    - **1.3.1** Information resources must be used in accordance with the SISW acceptable use guidelines.
    - **1.3.2** Processes must be implemented that facilitate the requirements for the acceptable use of information
resources in accordance with an established policy on human resources security.

**1.4** Standards must be established for the return of information resources upon termination of employment,
contract or agreement.
    - **1.4.1** Requirements must be established for the return of information resources upon termination or
change of employment, contract or agreement.

**1.5** Standards must be established for the classification, labeling, and handling of information resources.
    - **1.5.1** Resources must be handled in accordance with their established security classification as defined by
the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Data Classification, Sharing, and Handling Policy.

**1.6** Requirements must be established for the management of removable media in accordance with the
classification standards.
    - **1.6.1** Processes must be established for removal of sensitive data from information resources before the
disposal or reuse.
    - **1.6.2** Endpoint device management should take into consideration protection against malware, remote
disabling, end user analytics, encryption (see Cryptography Policy), and backup requirements (see Data Backup and
Recovery Policy).
    - **1.6.3** Endpoint devices should leverage virtual private networks (VPNs) when utilizing unsecure or public
networks.
    - **1.6.4** Internal wireless networks should be configured with secure protocols.

**1.7** Requirements must be implemented to facilitate defining data retention, decommissioning, and disposal
protection requirements consistent with contractual, legal, environmental, and business requirements.
    - **1.7.1** Information resources decommissioning and disposal methods must be defined based on the information
resources criticality.
    - **1.7.2** Processes must be implemented to facilitate defining data retention, decommissioning, and disposal
protection requirements consistent with contractual, legal, environmental, and business requirements.
    - **1.7.3** Prior to reuse or disposal of any asset, SISW must define requirements, inclusive of authorization, to
remove or securely overwrite any sensitive information that may be on devices or storage media.

**1.8** Processes must be implemented for the management of BYOD devices that access SISW information resources.

**1.9** Standards should be established to guide personnel on the types of software installations that are permitted
and what types of installations are prohibited on information resources.

**1.10** The use of resources shall be monitored, tuned and projections made of future capacity requirements to
ensure the required system performance.
