Applicable Product IDs: 
TC10408-XT – MultiSite
TC7030-XT – Dispatcher 
TC7003-XT – Teamcenter X Author

### Multisite Environment

tcTcX 2606 can support Supplier Connect, which requires Multi-site collaboration (henceforward referred to as Multisite). In 2606 Multisite can be deployed automatically via the pipeline deployment for TcX to TcX deployments. If manual steps are needed for a specific customer for some reason, the manual instructions are listed in the cookbooks for earlier releases. You may refer back to those for any manual instructions you may require. For TcX to On-Premises (On-Prem) connections please refer to that section of this cookbook for additional information. Installs will be the same for the TcX side but to connect it to the On-Prem customer, specific steps will need to be completed. Please refer to the Tc On Prem to TcX Configuration section.
