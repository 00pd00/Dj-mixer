### Clam AV timeout settings for large files upload

login with sudo su - tcx_user

Modify `/<tenantId>-<envType>/<tenantId>-<envType>/deploy/component/config/fmsmaster/fsc/fmsmaster_FSC_fmsmaster.xml`

Search below line (default timeout is 600)

`<quarantinevolume command="$SCAN_COMMAND $SCAN_FILE" root="/fms_network/quarantine" timeout="600"/>`

modify it as (change timeout to desired value)

`<quarantinevolume command="$SCAN_COMMAND $SCAN_FILE" root="/fms_network/quarantine" timeout="{placeholder}"/>`

After modifying fmsmaster_FSC_fmsmaster.xml restart fms pod using [workflow](../Day%20N%20Operations/Shutdown%20and%20restart%20workloads)
Select workload as "Teamcenter FSC"
