**1.12** Physical Security
Physical facilities must be protected from unauthorized physical access, environmental threats, damage, and
interruption in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Physical Security Policy.
  - **1.12.1** Security perimeter protection mechanisms must be implemented to reduce the risk of unauthorized
physical access.
  - **1.12.2** Access controls must be implemented to restrict physical access.
  - **1.12.3** Environmental protection controls must be implemented to ensure physical locations are protected
against external and environmental threats.
  - **1.12.4** Continuous monitoring of physical security must be implemented at all in-scope locations.
  - **1.12.5** A process must be defined for the removal of physical assets from all in-scope locations.
  - **1.12.6** The management of physical access keys must be implemented.

**1.13** Secure Software Development Lifecycle
Secure software development practices must be implemented to reduce the risk of software-related vulnerabilities
residing on information resources in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Secure
Development Policy.
  - **1.13.1** Security must be incorporated into all phases of the software development lifecycle.
  - **1.13.2** Source code must be maintained within secured and approved repositories.
  - **1.13.3** Training regarding secure architecture and engineering principles must be provided to software
developers.
  - **1.13.4** Requirements for secure coding principles must be established to ensure all software is securely
developed and security vulnerabilities are minimized.

**1.14** Third-Party Supplier Information Security
Information security risks associated with third party suppliers, inclusive of Information Communication Technology
(ICT) and cloud service providers (CSP), who access SISW information resources must be identified, assessed, and
managed in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Supplier Relationship Policy.
  - **1.14.1** Information security requirements must be documented and implemented to facilitate the selection
of third-party providers.
  - **1.14.2** Contracts with third-party providers must be signed and include information security and privacy
clauses.
  - **1.14.3** Products and services of third-party providers must be used in accordance with information security
policies.
  - **1.14.4** Information security reviews of existing third-party providers must be periodically performed.
  - **1.14.5** Termination of third-party providers or services must comply with information security and privacy
clauses defined in signed contracts.
  - **1.14.6** Information security requirements shall be determined and applied across all lCT services. Where
applicable, SISW shall require that ICT suppliers disseminate the security requirements and practices through
the supply chain.
  - **1.14.7** A Business impact analysis (BIA) must include continuity considerations during a disruption to ICT
services in accordance with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Business Continuity Management
Policy.
  - **1.14.8** SISW must determine the specific criteria for selecting, using, managing, and changing cloud service
providers for providing any or all parts of the services; particularly considerations must be applied for the
storage and processing of client information, including personal information, as part of the services.

**1.15** Vulnerability Management
Information resources must be maintained by identifying and remediating associated vulnerabilities in accordance
with the Siemens Industry Software (SISW) Saas Vulnerability and Patch Management Policy.
