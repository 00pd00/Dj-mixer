# Journey to ISO 27001 - SISW SaaS ISMS Policy Searchable Page-ocr - Page 28

- **1.2.1** Software applications, components and software libraries must be registered in SVM (Siemens
Vulnerability Monitoring Tool) to receive vulnerability notifications and triggers for fixing cloud environment
vulnerabilities.
    - **1.2.2** For software applications, components or software libraries which are not available in SVM Tool, a request
to add it must be placed.
    - **1.2.3** A vulnerability disclosure program must be established to share applicable critical information with
customers.
  - **1.3** Vulnerability scans and penetration tests must be performed periodically.
    - **1.3.1** Internal and external vulnerability scans must be performed on all information resources according to the
defined scanning criteria.
    - **1.3.2** External scans of any PCI environments must be validated by an Approved Scanning Vendor (ASV) on an
annual basis.
    - **1.3.3** Rescans must be performed if "critical" or "high" vulnerabilities classified are identified and are
subsequently remediated.
    - **1.3.4** Internal Information resources (not exposed to public access/internet) must be selected for penetration
testing based on the outcome of their Protection Concept evaluation.
      - **1.3.4.1** The selection process for assets to be included for penetration testing must also define the
frequency of the testing.
      - **1.3.4.2** Penetration tests for information assets not exposed to the internet must be performed at the
defined frequency and upon significant changes to the architecture.
    - **1.3.5** Penetration tests of information assets exposed to the internet must be performed at least annually and
upon significant changes to the network or architecture.
      - **1.3.5.1** Where information assets are exposed to the internet and process customer data, penetration
tests must be performed by an independent 3rd party approved by the CISO.
      - **1.3.5.2** During the initial launch of information assets exposed to the internet that process customer data,
a penetration test must be performed within 30 days.
      - **1.3.5.3** The approved independent vendors for performing penetration tests include Boaz Allen, Coalfire,
iVision, Optiv.
    - **1.3.6** A penetration re-test must be performed if a "critical" or "high" severity risk compromise for an attack
scenario is identified and subsequently remediated.
    - **1.3.7** Penetration tests must be performed according to an established penetration testing methodology that
considers:
• Coverage of the environment and other critical information resources
• Testing from outside and inside of the network
• Validation of segmentation controls
• Application and network layer testing requirements
• Consideration of previously experienced threats and vulnerabilities
• Retention requirements for testing results and remediation activities
    - **1.3.8** Personnel performing vulnerability scans and penetration tests must be organizationally independent
from personnel performing system administration activities.
  - **1.4** Vulnerability remediation and patch implementation must be performed within defined timelines.
    - **1.4.1** Identified vulnerabilities and patches must be managed by the Asset Manager based on the guidelines in
this policy.
    - **1.4.2** Patches must be implemented according to the Change Management Policy.
    - **1.4.3** Vendor-released patches with severity ratings of "critical" and "high" must be implemented within 30 and
60 days, respectively.
  - **1.5** Exceptions to vulnerability and patch management must be reviewed and approved.
    - **1.5.1** Exceptions process for vulnerability and patch management must define processes for documenting and
validating false positives for identified vulnerabilities
    - **1.5.2** Delayed or deferred actions for the remediation of vulnerabilities and implementation of patches that
cannot be addressed within required timelines require risk to be reviewed and approved.
    - **1.5.3** "Critical" or "High" vulnerabilities with remediations that are overdue require review and acceptance of
risk by the Business Segment Leader.
  - **1.6** Confidentiality of vulnerability and penetration test results must be identified and applied to all relevant
artifacts
