# Validating the Digital Reality Viewer installation
​​
Basic validation includes confirming that the Digital Reality Viewer application are available to the user, and Digital Reality Viewer can visualize 3D data successfully.

**Assumption:** You have the Digital Reality Viewer applications installed and can login aw client by tcsso normally.

1. In a browser, log in to AWC with the `TcXAdminUser` account.

1. create an item with name `drv_test` within your home folder, eg:

    ![Image](./create_test_item.png)

1. add a [jt file attachment](./allShapes.jt) for that item, eg:
  ![Image](./add_jt_attachment.png)

1. open item with Digital Reality Viewer application, ensure the Digital Reality Viewer can visualize 3D data successfully. eg:
  ![Image](./open_drv.png)
