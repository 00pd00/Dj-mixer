## Add roles to MyOrg

**Note:- These steps are applicable for TcX Premium only**

1. Log in to Teamcenter Active Workspace as dba.
2. Switch to the Active Admin workspace.
3. Navigate to "People" sublocation from the Launcher (Alt+Q).

   ![People Tool](./People_tool.png)

4. Select the "Organization" tab.
5. Navigate to the "MyOrg" group in the Organization tree.
6. Expand the "MyOrg" group and select the "Engineering" sub-group.
7. Right-click on the "Engineering" sub-group and select "Add Role".

   ![Add Role](./Add_role.png)

8. In the Add panel, select the "Search" tab.
9. Search for the role "Analyst" and click on the "Add" button.

   ![Add Analyst Role](./Analyst_role.png)

10. Add the required users to the "Analyst" role.
11. Also add the "Physical Test Engineer" role to the "Engineering" sub-group.

    ![Add Physical Test Engineer Role](./PhysicalTE_role.png)

12. Add the required users to the "Physical Test Engineer" role.
13. The roles appear under "Engineering" sub-group under "MyOrg" as shown below.

    ![MyOrg](./MyOrg.png)
