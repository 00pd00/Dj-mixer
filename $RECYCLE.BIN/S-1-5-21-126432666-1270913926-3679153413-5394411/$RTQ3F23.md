SISW Saas Supplier Relationship Policy requirements
  - **1.1** Identification of all Supplier Relationships
    - **1.1.1** All supplier services must be identified and categorized according to supplier type, significance and
criticality. This identification and categorization process should include any information communications
technology (ICT) capability and/or cloud service provider (CSP) and should be established prior to vendor
selection.
    - **1.1.2** Formal documentation of technical and organizational relationships must be maintained, covering the
roles, responsibilities and credentials of representatives of these suppliers.
  - **1.2** Supplier Selection
  - **1.2.1** All third-party service providers must go through a risk assessment prior to purchasing. The purpose
of this assessment is to determine the third-party's impact on the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of
Company data and processes, and determine whether the risk associated with using the vendor, service or
product is acceptable to the Company.
  - **1.2.2** SISW must determine criteria for selecting cloud service providers that provides considerations for:
• Merit of cloud service versus on-premises solutions
• Cloud specific security certifications or controls that the provider can attest to
• Compatibility with existing SISW cloud and non-cloud products and tools
• Availability and latency across distributed geographical locations
• Response and transparency for security related events
