
### Set-up Teamcenter X Operating SAM Account

This is one-time set-up per region across all customers. This account is created and owned for the Siemens managed services organization. There should be one SAM Account in every Teamcenter X region.

**This account is a Siemens internal-only account so it shall have access to SAM and SAMAuth Application Registration Console**  

#### Prerequisites

Following prerequisites must be met before you proceed with following the instructions provided in this document:

 
- All Siemens Managed Services staff who will be added to Teamcenter X Operating SAM account must already have a valid WebKey account.


The following information is needed to complete the instructions provided in this document:

- Detailed account information as needed by Zeus Account Request form in http://civ6w178:3000/assets/files/Zeus_Account_Request.xlsx
