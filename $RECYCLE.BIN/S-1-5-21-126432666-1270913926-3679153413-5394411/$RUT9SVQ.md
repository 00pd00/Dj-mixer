## TcX Upgrade from AW6.2 to AW2412 for SSR Visualization customers

Only current TcX customers using SSR (7020) can deploy SSR in AW6.2. SSR will no longer be available in TcX AW2412 and later versions.
Customers currently on SSR, upgrading to AW2412 must transition from SSR to CSR, which offers support for larger structures and additional features.
If further discussions on migrating from SSR to CSR is required with customer, please engage with Teamcenter Visualization Product Management (Ian Fisher, Todd Teske).
