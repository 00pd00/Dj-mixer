## Teamcenter X Operating SAM Account Set-up

### Teamcenter X Operating SAM Account Set-up Definitions

| **Term**                           | **Definition**                                                                                                                                      |
|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Teamcenter X Operating Users**    | Teamcenter X users that are part of Siemens managed services organization that is responsible to administer Teamcenter X on each customer environments.  |
| **Teamcenter X End-Users**          | Teamcenter X users that are part of customer organization that uses Teamcenter X on day-to-day basis                                          |
| **Teamcenter X Operating SAM Account** | A SAM account that is owned by Siemens managed services organization and consist of Siemens managed services staffs responsible      |
| **Teamcenter X Customer SAM Account**  | A SAM account that is owned by customer organization and consist of customer employees as well as Siemens managed services staffs with access to Teamcenter X customer environments |
