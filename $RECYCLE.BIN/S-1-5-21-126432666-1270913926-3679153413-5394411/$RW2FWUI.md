SISW Saas Information Security Human Resources Policy requirements

**1.1** The SISW Human Resources (HR) department is the central resource for employees to interface with the
organization and will be the point of contact for any concerns with regards to employment, security, safety and
benefits.
    - **1.1.1** SISW collects data required to screen candidates during the recruiting and hiring process, and
information required to fulfill the company's HR functions, deliver benefits, process payroll, and enable access
to SISW information systems. This information includes, but is not limited to, name, date of birth, address,
phone number, social security number, taxpayer identification number, age, marital status, and dependent
information. Country specific laws and limitations are taken into account for the screening of new hires.
    - **1.1.2** SISW uses third-party systems as part of the HR function; recruiting, on boarding, payroll, benefits,
wellness, and retention. These third-party systems provide secure hosting of SISW data, and SISW retains full
rights as the data controller on all these systems.
    - **1.1.3** The SISW HR will make the final determination for employment, and employment may be subject to
additional verification or screening processes as determined by HR, and as permitted by applicable law.

**1.2** SISW will communicate to employees, all terms and agreements with regards to employment, via HR and
employees must acknowledge receipt at the time of employment, and during any subsequent updates, as directed
by HR. The contractual obligations may reflect SISW's policies for information security while also emphasizing:
• Employee's requirements to protect and safeguard SISW assets with respect to security, availability, and
confidentiality
• Employee's legal responsibilities and rights (e.g., regarding intellectual property, workplace privacy, etc.)
• The corrective action process for remediating violations of SISW's security requirements

**1.3** Managers at all levels of the organization shall be engaged in driving security within the organization and shall
foster behavior and policy that drive and encourage all employees to apply security in accordance with SISW's
established policies and procedures. Managers, at all levels of the organization, shall:
• Serve as role models for their teams, demonstrating support for, and proper compliance with, information
security policies, procedures and control
• Ensure that their teams continue to have the appropriate resources, skills and qualifications necessary to
complete their work

**1.4** SISW HR provides Siemens Business Conduct Guidelines and SISW Company Directives that serve as guidelines
for performing work within SISW's systems and must always be observed by all employees. Any SISW assets that
may be issued to or used by employees should be used in accordance with these policies and employees must
always make reasonable efforts to safeguard the asset.

**1.5** SISW provides for adequate training and awareness to all its employees on compliance with applicable
requirements, and their participation in the effort. The training shall cover:
• Management's commitment to information security
• The importance of understanding and complying with all applicable information security rules and obligations
found in policies, procedures, standards, laws, regulations and contracts
• How to perform any needed role-specific information security duties
• Personal accountability for one's actions/inaction, as well as general responsibilities for securing and protecting
information
