##  How to Subscribe

1. **Click on any channel link above**
2. **Join the team** if you're not already a member
3. **Turn on notifications** for important announcements:
   - Click the channel name
   - Select "Channel notifications"
   - Choose "All new posts" or "Mentions & replies"
