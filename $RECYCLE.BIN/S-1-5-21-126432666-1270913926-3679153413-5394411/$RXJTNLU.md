# Journey to ISO 27001 - SISW SaaS ISMS Policy Searchable Page-ocr

**1.1** SISW's ISMS has been developed as a system that recognizes the need for organizational compliance with strong
security principles, guided by ISO/IEC 27001:2022 requirements.
    - **1.1.1** SISW will recognize and comply with all applicable local legislative, statutory, regulatory requirements
in addition to contractual requirements established by interested parties.
      - **1.1.1.1** The central Legal, Data Privacy, and Compliance organizations will review applicable
local legislative, statutory, regulatory requirements and contractual requirements to remain
current with changing laws and regulations. They will document and issue guidance as required
by law supported by the "Privacy by Design" tool and process.
    - **1.1.2** Where applicable, considerations to affected laws and regulations should be taken into account when
the organization:
• Conducts business in other countries
• Transfers information over jurisdictional borders
• Uses products and services from other countries
    - **1.1.3** SISW must identify and comply with specific legal requirements regarding cryptography such as the
use of cryptography across international borders and the import and export of hardware and software that
perform cryptographic functions.
    - **1.1.4** SISW must include information security requirements within contracts with customers, third-party
suppliers, and insurance companies.

**1.2** SISW purchases and licenses software from a variety of sources. Any duplication of software except as
permitted by related license agreements is a violation of copyright law. Installing unauthorized software on a
Corporate Environment or Production Environment system can lead to potential system failures, system
degradation, or security incidents and data loss, and is therefore strictly restricted per the Employee Manual.

**1.3** Software license requirements are communicated to all end users as required.

**1.4** SISW must publish procedures for intellectual property rights compliance that includes maintaining
appropriate license conditions and the disposal or transfer of software to others.

**1.5** SISW recognizes three types of software licenses that may be in use within the ISMS:
• Subscription based: This model provides for on-demand mode of licensing and will hence enforce any
compliance requirements inherently. Such licenses are not considered SISW property and will be strictly
returned to the owner upon termination.
• Fixed licenses: Fixed licenses are designed for use with a single asset and may have restricted rules towards
reuse and transfer. SISW will abide by all governing acceptable use policies associated with such licenses and
users will need to verify with Legal before making copies or reusing software associated with such licenses.
• Open Source: Open source software and licenses may be used in the SISW environment and in the production
of SISW assets only with the prior approval of Legal. Such licenses are closely monitored by SISW for fair use
terms and users will be advised on the appropriate restrictions by SISW management team.

**1.6** Procedures shall be documented, and communicated to relevant personnel, regarding the preservation of PII
inclusive of defined roles and responsibilities regarding the handling of PII.
    - **1.6.1** SISW must comply with all national and local laws and regulations regarding the preservation of
privacy and protection of PII.

**1.7** SISW has documented operational standards and procedures and made these available to all employees to
serve as a reference for operating within the ISMS.

**1.8** SISW employees have designated roles and responsibilities both within the ISMS and their job functions that
help guide them on their contribution towards the success of the ISMS.

**1.9** SISW provides for adequate training and awareness to its employees on compliance with applicable
requirements, and their participation in the effort.
