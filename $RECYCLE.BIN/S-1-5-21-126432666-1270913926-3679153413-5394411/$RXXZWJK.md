## Scope

Rhapsody Connector for Teamcenter requires the following environment to work.

**Operating system:**
- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)

**Teamcenter software versions:**
- Teamcenter 2412
- Teamcenter 2406

**Rhapsody software versions:**
- Rhapsody Version 2410
- Teamcenter integration for Rhapsody v2412 with CIS SDK 2412
