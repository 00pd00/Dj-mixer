# Overview

Welcome! This document provides clear steps for updating your TcX product tier. Please read all information carefully before beginning the process.

---

## Important Note

- **Purpose**: Use this guide only to update your TcX product tier (for example, from TcX Standard to TcX Advanced or from TcX Standard to TcX Premium) within the same TcX Version.
- **Limitations**: This cookbook **does not support** installing additional modules or making configuration changes. Attempting these actions is discouraged.

---

## Prerequisites

Before starting the tier update process, ensure you have the following:

1. Access to [Ansible Tower](https://ansible-tcx-prod.plmcloudsolutions.com/)
2. Access to Xcelerator [Admin Console](https://cloud.sw.siemens.com/)

---

If you meet the prerequisites and are updating your TcX product tier for the same version, proceed confidently with the steps in this guide.
